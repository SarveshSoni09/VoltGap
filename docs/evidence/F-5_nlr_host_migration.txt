developer.nrel.gov retirement - evidence
retrieved 2026-08-19 UTC

DNS resolution, public resolver 1.1.1.1:
  $ dig +short @1.1.1.1 developer.nrel.gov
  (no record)
  $ dig +short @1.1.1.1 developer.nlr.gov
  (resolves)

Published statement: the NREL developer network moved to developer.nlr.gov and the
developer.nrel.gov domain was retired on 29 May 2026.
Source: https://developer.nlr.gov/docs/transportation/alt-fuel-stations-v1/

Impact on delivered project material:
  SETUP.md section 3 directs the operator to developer.nrel.gov/signup for the free
  NREL Developer key. That URL no longer resolves. Keys must be obtained from
  developer.nlr.gov.
