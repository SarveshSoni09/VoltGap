Domain rule G9 factual discrepancy - evidence
retrieved 2026-08-19 UTC

CLAUDE.md section 5, domain rule G9 states:
  "State registration counts have inconsistent reporting vintages across states.
   Oregon reports 6,436, below Kansas at 11,271 and Iowa at 9,031, which is
   implausible. Flag outliers by comparing per-capita rates against neighboring
   states and mark low-confidence states in the data."

data/seed/MANIFEST.md repeats the claim:
  "Oregon reports 6,436, which falls below Kansas (11,271), Iowa (9,031), and Maine
   (7,377). That ordering is implausible and indicates inconsistent reporting
   vintages across states."

Actual delivered values, data/seed/EV_Registration_Counts_by_State.csv
(SHA-256 4c72eeace1defeddf7dddeaedf4f65ffff970b4c2ce8090184fd6408076bb7ad):
  Oregon    64,361
  Kansas    11,271
  Iowa       9,031
  Maine      7,377
  Total   3,555,445  (equals the sum of the 51 jurisdiction rows exactly)

Oregon is 64,361, not 6,436. The stated value looks like a truncation of the real one.
At 64,361 the ordering is entirely plausible and the anomaly G9 describes does not
exist in the delivered file.

The general claim was also tested. Rounding every delivered value half-up to the
nearest 100 reproduces the AFDC 2023 vintage table for 51 of 51 jurisdictions:
  Oregon 64,361 -> 64,400 = AFDC 2023 Oregon 64,400
  Kansas 11,271 -> 11,300 = AFDC 2023 Kansas 11,300
  Iowa    9,031 ->  9,000 = AFDC 2023 Iowa    9,000
  California 1,256,646 -> 1,256,600 = AFDC 2023 California 1,256,600
The file is therefore a single consistent 2023 vintage across all states, which
contradicts G9's general claim of inconsistent reporting vintages across states.

Comparison against adjacent AFDC vintages: 0 of 51 half-up matches against 2022,
0 of 51 against 2024. The match is specific to 2023.
