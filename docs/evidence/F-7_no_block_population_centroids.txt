Bearing on the specification: CLAUDE.md section 7.5 permits "population-weighted
centroids OR block-level allocation"; section 7.6 requires "block-level population
weights, not area weights". The ready-made population-weighted product stops at block
group. Genuine block-level weighting must be constructed from TIGER blocks joined to
P.L. 94-171 block population. Both paths are free; the choice is a Phase 2 decision.
